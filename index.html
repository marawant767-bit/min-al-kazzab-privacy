Privacy Policy – مين الكذاب؟

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game مين الكذاب؟ (“the Game”) handles information.

1. Information We Collect

The Game is designed to work without requiring an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone recordings, or payment information.

2. Game Data

Game-related information may be stored locally on your device when needed for the Game to function. We do not intentionally upload this locally stored game information to our own servers.

3. Internet Access

The current Android package may request Internet access for technical functionality. The Game itself does not intentionally use the Internet to collect personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

The Android build may contain third-party Android libraries included by the app-building framework. In the current configuration, advertising features are disabled and no advertising identifier or advertising unit is configured by the Game.

If third-party services are enabled in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when those services are introduced.

5. Children’s Privacy

The Game is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the Game does not require an account or intentionally collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect such information through the Game.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Game’s features or data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will be changed when this happens.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the Game, please contact the developer through the distribution platform where the Game was downloaded.
